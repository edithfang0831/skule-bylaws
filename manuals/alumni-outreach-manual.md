# For Candidates

## Role Summary
1. The Alumni Outreach Director creates initiatives to connect current U of T undergraduate engineering students with alumni.

## General Responsibilities
1. The Alumni Outreach Director works closely with U of T Engineering Office of Advancement to build and strengthen student body's connection with alumni.
2. The Alumni Outreach Director plans events (e.g. panels, resume review, workshops, etc) to increase alumni's engagement within the SKULE community.

## Required Qualifications
1. The Alumni Outreach Director must be able to communicate well and professionally in writing and orally, including with their team, to the public at events like clubs fair, panels, etc.
   

## Useful Qualifications
1. Prior experience in event planning and hosting is a plus.
2. Time management and organization, especially adherence to contract deadlines, is an asset.

## Role Takeaways
1. A Alumni Outreach Director missing qualifications listed in section 0.3 may gain them by exercising the role.
2. The Alumni Outreach Director role is useful experience for a prospective candidate for [President](president-manual.md) to have.

# Summary and Structure

## Role Summary
1. The Alumni Outreach Director creates initiatives to connect current U of T undergraduate engineering students with alumni.

## Transition
1. The Alumni Outreach Director shall transition their successor as described in the [Transition Policy](../policies/transition-policy.md).

## Executive Structure
1. Alumni Communication Team
2. Student Communication Team
3. Event Organization Team
4. Webmaster

## Executive Responsibilities
1. Alumni Communication Coordinator
- Communicate with the Engineering Alumni Office and seek other opportunities to help recruit alumni for events and initiatives
- Develop and maintain relationships with alumni
- Collect and analyze event feedback from alumni
2. Student Communication Coordinator
- Communicate with current undergraduate students to increase and maintain event engagement
- Collect and analyze event feedback from students
3. Marketing Coordinator
- Create social media (e.g. Instagram and LinkedIn) graphics/posts for events
- Manage social accounts and increase social media presence
- Send out monthly newsletters to students and alumni
- Prior graphic design/ marketing experience is preferred but not required
4. Event Coordinators (x2)
- Plan event timelines, book venues, arrange food/beverages, manage attendee lists, etc.
- Should be organized, creative and have good time management
= Prior experience with event planning/budgeting is preferred, but not required
5. Webmaster
- Create and maintain the website for Skule Alumni Outreach
- Cooperate with the marketing and communication coordinators to draft website content
- Prior web design experience is required

## Reporting Structure
1. The Alumni Outreach Director is overseen by the [President](president-manual.md).

# Manual of Responsibilities

## Ongoing Responsibilities
1. Monthly meeting with the Office of Advancement
2. Planning and executing events engaging students with alumni
