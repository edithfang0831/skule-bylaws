# For Candidates

## Role Summary
1. The Sponsorship Director is responsible for securing parterships and discounts for SKULE events such as grad balls, cannon balls, dinner dances, etc.

## General Responsibilities
1. Reach out to potential sponsors for partnerships.
2. Maintaining exisiting partnerships.
3. Develop sponsorship packages.

## Required Qualifications
1. Strong communication and organization skills.
2. Prior experience with outreach and creating sponsorship packages - Should have a clear understanding of effective outreach strategies for this role and what to include in a sponsorship package.

## Useful Qualifications
1. Organization and experience talking to discipline clubs cross-functionally.

## Role Takeaways
1. Better understanding of EngSoc's finance portfolio
2. Improve organization and communication skills - Sponsorship may be secured at different time during a year and some are renewable
